Evaluating QEC primitives on quantum processors, at large width and depth

Every round of error correction measures ancillas in the middle of the circuit and feeds the outcomes forward. We benchmark that cycle on IBM, IQM and Quantinuum processors, from 1D chains to the checks of surface, colour and qLDPC codes.
