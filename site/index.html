How well do quantum processors run the primitive error correction is built on?

Quantum error correction rests on one operation: measure an ancilla mid-circuit and feed the outcome forward. We test it three ways on the same devices: as the gadget inside a linear-ramp QAOA on 1D chains of growing length, on the check Hamiltonians of QEC codes written in three frames, and as a real surface-code memory of |0⟩L and |+⟩L.

    Mid-circuit measurement on 1D chains

    An Ising chain of N spins whose every coupling is applied through an ancilla: entangle, rotate, measure it mid-circuit, correct the two neighbours by feed-forward. Longer chains mean more gadgets per layer; deeper circuits mean more layers. The direct reference applies the same couplings with two-qubit gates and no ancilla.

    QEC structures in three frames

    The checks of a surface, colour or qLDPC code read as a Hamiltonian, each measured through its own ancilla: one LR-QAOA layer is one round of syndrome extraction. The Z frame writes every check as Z…Z, the X frame as X…X, and the XZ frame keeps each check in the basis the code gives it, as a memory experiment does, under a Y mixer. All three are the same algorithm with the same noiseless answer, so where they differ on a device, the device is telling them apart.

    Method

    Every LR-QAOA benchmark here uses fixed angles on a linear ramp (γ rises and β falls over p layers, slope Δ/p, Δ = 0.5), so it runs identically on every device. Each term of the Hamiltonian is applied by the mid-circuit-measurement gadget: the ancilla is entangled with the term's data qubits, rotated, measured, and its outcome corrects the data by feed-forward.

    Noiselessly the approximation ratio r grows with p; on a device noise pulls it back towards random guessing. A device that holds r above random at larger N and deeper p runs the primitive better.

    The memory experiment is decoded with minimum-weight perfect matching on the detector error model of the same circuit. On IQM only the last round's syndrome is returned, and such runs are decoded from that record; they are marked in the table.

    Approximation ratio r
    (Emax − ⟨E⟩) / (Emax − Eopt): 1 is the optimum, ½ is a uniformly random sampler for these problems.
    Random + 3σ
    The exact mean of r for random bitstrings plus three times its shot-noise spread at the shots taken.
    Effective ratio reff
    (r − t) / (1 − t), with t = random + 3σ. Above 0: better than random guessing by more than 3σ.
    Noiseless line
    The exact r of the same circuit without noise, where it can be computed.
    Logical error probability PL
    The fraction of shots the decoder gets wrong after R rounds.
